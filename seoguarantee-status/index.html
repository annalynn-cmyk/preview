The two findings worth the exercise

A quality check that could not fail. The substance test compared marked text against unmarked text, so copy repeated on 54 pages counted as unique. Rerun on identical bytes after the fix, 63 pages fell below the floor. Every page has been remeasured since.

The pattern behind all four

Each was a rule written to prevent a real failure: pages contradicting each other, pages being unreachable, the phrase going missing from titles. They were caught by reading the site, not by the checks, which is the argument for reviewing pages by hand at every stage rather than trusting the measurements.

Are they different enough from each other?

Measured, not assumed. Every page is compared against every other page of its kind, using the wording rather than the layout, and two tests have to pass before a page is allowed to go live.

TestWhat it requiresCurrent result
DifferenceNo two pages of the same kind may share more than half their wordingIndustry pages in the menus: 1 pair of 1,035 over the line. States: 0 of 1,225. Combinations: 0 of 3
SubstanceAt least a third of a page must be wording that appears on no other page at all2 of the 46 linked industry pages fall short. States: none

The one industry pair over the line is dermatology and plastic surgery. They are recorded as an exception with the reason: the federal statistics use a single code for both, so they legitimately share a figure, and neither has its own search capture yet. The exception is reviewed the moment either gains one.

The measurement is stricter than it sounds in one respect and looser in another. Wording that is required to be identical, the guarantee line and the source note under a figure, is excluded before comparing, because a fixed commitment repeated accurately is not duplication. Everything else counts, including anything a writer might reach for out of habit. That is how the two template faults found in testing were caught.

On the standard these are being held to

Search engines object to pages generated at scale that add nothing, and to sets of near-identical pages made to catch every variation of a search. The defence is not length or wording: it is that each page carries information that exists nowhere else on the site, gathered for that page. A dated record of what page one looks like for plumbers in Las Vegas is that. A paragraph rewritten to sound different is not.

This is why pages are refused rather than padded, and why the checks measure distinct content rather than word count.

The opening this strategy is built on

A search for "guaranteed seo houston" returned a page that never uses the word guarantee. The agencies that rank for city and industry searches do not mention one, and the ones that do are thin. Nobody occupies both, which is the gap the whole phrase map is aimed at.

The brand name helps rather than hinders: because it contains the phrase, every page carries it in the title honestly, without a keyword being inserted anywhere it does not belong.

Tomorrow's priority: the 32 states with nothing to say

The service is sold nationwide, so all 50 states need a page worth reading. 18 have one today, and only because each has a city beneath it. The other 32 carry business figures and nothing else, which made them the thinnest pages in the test. A nationwide claim with 32 empty state pages undercuts itself.

The answer is not 32 separate research projects. One source does most of the work.

One board per state, nine industries unlocked

Each state's contractor licensing board covers nine of our trades at once: heating and air conditioning, plumbing, roofing, electrical, general contracting, painting, fencing, concrete and solar. The rules differ genuinely between states, some license, some only register, some leave it to counties, and the advertising rules differ with them. That difference is the content, and it cannot be templated.

It is the highest-yield research in the project: one board fills a section on that state's page and on nine industry pages at the same time.

The decision in front of us

Not whether the approach works. It does, and the gates prove it page by page. The decision is how much evidence-gathering to fund, and in what order, because that is what sets the pace.

The cheapest useful outcome is a launch of roughly 25 pages: the core pages, Las Vegas, the three industries that already have their evidence, and the guarantee articles. That targets the phrases that matter most, the bare term and the home market, and everything else follows in waves behind it.
