The rules

Nevada requires your license number on your website

  • NRS 624.720(7)(a) Advertising is defined broadly and names the Internet and directory listings under a contractor heading.
  • NRS 624.720(3) It is unlawful for a licensed contractor to publish a false or misleading statement of material fact intended to induce someone to use their services.
  • NRS 624.720(3) All advertising by a licensed contractor must include the company name and the contractor's license number, and the board names the website among the places it must appear. Same rule as HVAC and contracting; all three sit under the Nevada State Contractors Board.
  • NSCB FAQ Central Plumbing work requires a license regardless of the small-repair threshold that exempts minor work. A plumbing site cannot use handyman framing.
  • NRS 624.720(7)(a): Directory profiles count as advertising, so the license number has to match across them.
  • NRS 624.720(3): Response-time and savings claims need to be supportable.

Sources: official board page; official statute. Retrieved 2026-09-18.
