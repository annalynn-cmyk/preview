The numbers

135,548 businesses in Pennsylvania, across the trades we work in

  • contracting companies: 18,285 Specialty trade contractors (NAICS 238). The figure counts every specialty trade, not contracting companies alone.
  • restaurants: 9,433 Full-service restaurants (NAICS 722511). Excludes limited-service restaurants, which are 722513.
  • churches: 9,420 Religious organizations (NAICS 813110). Counts congregations with paid employees only, so it understates the number of churches.
  • dermatology practices: 7,854 Offices of physicians (NAICS 621111). Dermatology has no separate code and is counted inside this figure.
  • plastic surgery practices: 7,854 Offices of physicians (NAICS 621111). Plastic surgery has no separate code and is counted inside this figure.

Source: US Census Bureau, County Business Patterns 2023, published on 2017 NAICS. County Business Patterns counts establishments with paid employees, so sole proprietors without employees are not in it. Population: US Census Bureau, Vintage 2024 Population Estimates (NST-EST2024-ALLDATA), 2023 estimate.

That is 18,285 contracting companies for 13,017,721 people, about one for every 712 residents in Pennsylvania.

The five above are 39% of the 135,548 businesses we count in Pennsylvania, and the other 41 trades share the rest.
