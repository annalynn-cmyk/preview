Nevada SEO Guarantee

STATE LANDSCAPE

Guaranteed SEO in Nevada

Nevada SEO is one market wearing a state's clothing. The Las Vegas valley holds most of the population and most of the businesses, so for the great majority of trades, ranking in Nevada means ranking in Las Vegas, with Reno a distant second. That is unusual, and it simplifies the work: there is one page to win rather than five.

Winning it is another matter. On one afternoon in September we captured six searches across the building trades here, with the location set to Las Vegas and personalisation off. Every one returned a map block sitting above the first ordinary result, and every one had a directory in the top ten. In air conditioning there were three paid adverts above even the map. In plumbing, three searches on the same afternoon produced three different pages: four adverts on one, none at all on another.

There are 631 plumbing and air conditioning establishments in this metro competing for five places in that map block.

What Nevada requires on your website

This is the part that catches most Nevada businesses out, and it is not a marketing question.

All advertising by a licensed contractor must include the name of the contractor's company and the number of the contractor's licence. NRS 624.720(4). The State Contractors Board names the website explicitly among the places that applies. The number belongs on the site, not only on the contact page, and missing it is a violation rather than a style choice.

The statute defines advertising broadly. It names signs, cards, markings on a vehicle, buildings, newspapers, magazines, airway transmission, the Internet, and any directory under a contractor heading. NRS 624.720(6)(a). So your Yelp and Angi profiles are advertising, and the number has to be consistent across all of them. A number that is right on the website and wrong on a directory is still wrong.

Three more rules follow from the same section and each one touches the page directly.

It is unlawful to advertise as a contractor without a licence in the appropriate classification, and an unlicensed person who advertises construction work must state in the advertisement that they are not licensed. NRS 624.720(1) and (2). The services your site lists have to sit inside what your licence covers.

It is unlawful to advertise using a licence number that does not correspond to a valid licence issued to you. NRS 624.720(5). A number carried over from a previous entity, or a subcontractor using the main contractor's number, is a violation on the page itself.

And it is unlawful to disseminate, as part of any advertising, a false or misleading statement of material fact intended to induce someone to use your services. NRS 624.720(3). Response times, savings claims and guarantees of your own all have to be supportable.

Outside construction the boards differ. A dental practice in Nevada cannot describe itself in a way that implies a specialty it does not hold, which decides the wording of a page before anyone optimises it.

Which trades Nevada licenses, and which it does not

Nevada is simpler than most states here, and that is worth knowing because several neighbouring states are not.

The building trades sit under one body, the State Contractors Board: air conditioning, plumbing, roofing, electrical, general contracting, painting, concrete and fencing all share the same advertising rule. In Texas, by contrast, there is no statewide general contractor licence at all and roofing is unlicensed, so a Texas roofer advertising as state licensed is claiming something that does not exist. In Colorado, general contracting is licensed city by city while plumbing and electrical are licensed by the state.

Heating and air conditioning work, and plumbing work, require a licence regardless of the small-repair threshold that exempts minor work in some trades. So an HVAC or plumbing site in this state cannot use handyman framing, and a site that does is inviting a question it does not want asked.

Nevada also has a reciprocal arrangement with Arizona, which matters more here than the distance suggests: a business working both sides of that border is common in this market.

Your postal address may not be your jurisdiction

Clark County contains six separate business licensing jurisdictions: unincorporated Clark County, the City of Las Vegas, Henderson, North Las Vegas, Boulder City and Mesquite. Each has its own application, fee schedule, renewal system and local rules, and a business serving the whole valley can need as many as five licences.

Now the part that matters for search. A Las Vegas mailing address usually is not the City of Las Vegas. A large share of the valley has a Las Vegas postal address and sits in unincorporated Clark County. Paradise, Spring Valley, Enterprise, Sunrise Manor, Whitney and Winchester are generally unincorporated. So is the Strip.

That is a licensing problem and a visibility problem at the same time. The city in your postal address, the jurisdiction that licensed you, and the places your customers actually search from may be three different things. If your profile, your citations and your pages disagree about where you are, they are disagreeing about the thing local search is built on.

There is one piece of relief, and it applies only to contractors. A contractor licensed under NRS 624 can hold a multi-jurisdictional licence issued by a primary jurisdiction that also authorises work in the participating ones: Henderson, Las Vegas, North Las Vegas and unincorporated Clark County. Boulder City and Mesquite are not participating. And the order matters: the state licence comes first, then the local authorisation.

How crowded this market is

One figure we can give now, because roofing is separately coded. Nevada has one of the lowest roofing establishment densities in the country, at roughly 3.5 per hundred thousand residents against a national figure more than twice that. Fewer competitors, and the same map with the same number of places in it. Where the field is thin, the work is faster and the guarantee is easier to write.

Where the people are

Two metros carry this state. The Las Vegas valley holds the great majority of the population and the businesses; Reno and Sparks hold most of the rest; everything else is small and far apart.

That has a practical consequence for how the work is scoped. Local results are weighted by where the searcher is standing, so a business in Henderson does not appear the same way to someone in Summerlin, and neither of them is competing with Reno at all. We draw the keyword set and the rank grid around the area you actually serve rather than around the state line, and the guarantee is written to that grid.

The state rule is the same across the building trades here. What differs is the page you are competing on.

Air conditioning and HVAC. Our capture returned three paid adverts, then a map of five companies, then Yelp and Angi, before the first ordinary result. The map decides this trade, and the map is fed by a profile most companies have not touched since it was set up.

Plumbing. Three plumbing searches on one afternoon produced three different pages: four adverts on one, none at all on another. Which phrase you are found for decides how much of the page you reach, and emergency work is where the auction is most expensive.

Roofing. One of the lowest densities in the country, as above. The opening is real, and the same licence rule applies to the site.

Contracting companies. Advertising without a licence in the right classification is unlawful, and so is advertising with a number that does not match a valid licence. The services list on the page has to sit inside the classification.

Dental. A practice may not describe itself in a way that implies a specialty it does not hold, which decides the wording before anyone optimises it. Different board, same principle.

Electrical. Same board and the same rule as the other building trades, and Google's Guaranteed badge carries more weight in this trade than most, because a homeowner is letting someone work on their wiring.
