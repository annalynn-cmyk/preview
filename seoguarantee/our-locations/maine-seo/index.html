  • contracting companies: 3,712 Specialty trade contractors (NAICS 238). The figure counts every specialty trade, not contracting companies alone.
  • restaurants: 1,446 Full-service restaurants (NAICS 722511). Excludes limited-service restaurants, which are 722513.
  • general contractors and remodelers: 1,074 Residential remodelers (NAICS 236118). Residential work only.
  • landscaping and lawn care companies: 964
  • tree services: 964 Landscaping services (NAICS 561730). Tree services are counted inside this figure.

Source: US Census Bureau, County Business Patterns 2023, published on 2017 NAICS. County Business Patterns counts establishments with paid employees, so sole proprietors without employees are not in it. Population: US Census Bureau, Vintage 2024 Population Estimates (NST-EST2024-ALLDATA), 2023 estimate.

That is 3,712 contracting companies for 1,399,646 people, about one for every 377 residents in Maine.
