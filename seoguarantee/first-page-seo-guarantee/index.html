How it works

The First Page SEO Guarantee

What the guarantee covers, how the phrases are chosen, and what it does not promise.

The guarantee

We put the key phrases in your contract on the first page of Google.

It is offered only after an assessment, and only to businesses that qualify.

Scope

What the guarantee covers

The guarantee is written against first page rankings on the target key phrases named in your contract. The scope is up to 50% of the contracted key phrases reach page one, depending on competition.

Key phrase selection is mutual: the agency proposes the key phrases, the client approves them before work starts. You are not handed a list after the fact.

The published windows are 6 month and 12 month, depending on the agreement.

Limits

What it does not promise

What we do not promise: a number one position. An agency that promises one exact position is promising a result nobody controls.

How hard first page is in your market depends on domain lifespan, existing backlinks and citations and current domain authority. That is what the assessment measures, and it is why the guarantee is offered only after an assessment, and only to businesses that qualify.
