SEO Suite: three kinds of search, one service

Most agencies sell one of these and call it SEO. All three are in every engagement, because a business that wins one and loses the other two is still missing most of the page. The guarantee covers all three.

Local SEO

The map results. Your Google Business Profile, the citations behind it, and the reviews that sit above every ordinary result in a local trade. In the searches we captured in Las Vegas, a map block appeared above the first organic result every time.

Organic search

The rankings everybody means by SEO. What the pages are built on, what sits between you and the top of them, and the technical work that decides whether a search engine can read the site at all.

AI search

The answers assistants give when nobody scrolls. A different surface with different rules, and increasingly the first thing a customer reads.

All three, in one service

Website design, development and management

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the website really included?

It comes with the guarantee rather than on top of it, with no separate build fee and no maintenance retainer. You are billed for one thing.

Do you outsource any of it?

No. Everything is done in one house, which is what makes the guarantee possible: we cannot promise an outcome we do not control.

What is actually guaranteed?

First page rankings on the key phrases named in your contract, within six or twelve months depending on the agreement, covering up to half of those phrases depending on how competitive your market is. We propose the phrases, you approve them before work starts.

Does the guarantee cover local and AI search as well as ordinary rankings?

It covers all three. Ask us during the audit what that looks like for your market, because a map result and an AI answer are different surfaces from an organic position.

Can you guarantee the number one spot?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. Nobody controls the exact position of a result. Page one is something we can work to and measure against.
