Organic SEO Services: Earning The Position Nobody Can Buy

The ordinary results, below the adverts and below the map. These are the ones nobody can pay for, which is what makes them worth having and why they take the longest to win. Organic SEO is the work that puts you there and keeps you there.

First page rankings on the key phrases you approve, guaranteed.

The slowest surface to win, and the hardest to lose

The map can change in weeks, because most of the early work there is correcting things that are simply wrong. An AI answer can change the day a model updates. The ordinary rankings move more slowly than either, and that cuts both ways.

Getting there takes longer. Everyone ahead of you has been working at it too, some of them for a decade, and the site itself has to be capable of ranking before anything else matters.

But a position earned in the ordinary results is the most durable thing you can hold in search. It cannot be bought, so a competitor cannot outspend you for it overnight, and it does not disappear when a budget stops. That durability is the whole argument for this part of the work.

What actually decides where you rank

Four things, and three of them are about your own site.

The phrases the site is built for

Not the ones you would like to rank for, the ones your customers type. Research first, then a list we propose and you approve, then pages built for that list. A site competing for phrases nobody searches is working hard in an empty room.

Whether a search engine can read the site

Structure, headings, titles and descriptions, internal links, speed, and how it behaves on a phone. None of this is visible to the owner and all of it is visible to a search engine, which is why a site can look finished and rank nowhere.

Whether the page answers the question

Content that says something specific, sourced where it needs to be. A page that restates what every competitor has already written gives a search engine no reason to prefer it, and increasingly no reason for an AI to quote it either.

Authority, which is the slow one

How old the domain is, what links and citations point at it, and what the competition already has. This is the factor that decides whether page one in your market is a six month job or a twelve month one, and it is the first thing the audit measures.

What we do

All of it in the same contract as the rest of the SEO, and none of it billed separately.

Keyword research and the phrase list

What your customers actually type, what each phrase is worth, and which ones are winnable in your market. We propose the list, you approve it before work starts, and the contract is written against it.

Technical work

Canonicals and internal linking, duplicate content, images and code, mobile and desktop treated as equally primary, and Google Search Console set up properly so the site's own data is available rather than guessed at.

On-page work

Titles, descriptions and headings written for the phrases in your contract rather than stuffed with them, and pages structured so the thing a visitor came for is the thing they find first.

Content

Pages that answer the questions people are actually asking, written to be read as well as to rank. We research the questions rather than inventing them.

Authority

Quality links, content that earns citation, and the press work behind it. What we do not do is buy links, spin articles or submit your site to a hundred directories nobody reads. That work was sold for years, and it is why some sites cannot rank now.

You are competing with a known quantity

This part is measurable before anyone signs anything.

There are 631 plumbing and air conditioning establishments in the Las Vegas metro, and 111,207 nationally. County Business Patterns 2023, the most recent year published, counting businesses with paid employees.

Not all of them are competing for the same phrases, and most of them are not competing well. The audit narrows that number to the businesses actually holding the positions you want, looks at how old their domains are and what links they carry, and turns "the market is competitive" into a figure we are willing to write a contract against.

How the guarantee applies here

This is the surface the guarantee was written for. First page rankings on the key phrases named in your contract, within six or twelve months depending on the agreement, covering up to half of those phrases depending on how competitive your market turns out to be. We propose the phrases, you approve them before work starts.

What we will not promise is a specific position. Nobody controls where a result lands, and an agency promising the number one spot is promising something outside its control. Page one is a position we can work to and measure against.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does organic SEO cost?

There is no fixed price list, and an agency with one is quoting before it has looked. It is not sold separately in any case: local, organic and AI search are one service here. What your market takes depends on your domain's age, the links and citations you already have, and who is ahead of you now. You get the number after the audit.

How long does it take?

Six months or twelve, set in the agreement, and which one depends on what the audit finds rather than on what anyone would like to hear. Authority is the factor that usually decides it.

Do I choose the keywords, or do you?

Both. We research what your customers type and propose the list. You approve every phrase before work starts, which is the part most guarantees leave out.

What if my site is old, or badly built?

Then it is part of the job rather than an obstacle to it, and the website is in the same contract. The audit says whether the existing site can be improved or should be rebuilt, and that answer affects the window rather than the price of a separate project.

Can you guarantee the number one position?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. Positions are produced by an algorithm that weighs the searcher's location among other things, so two people searching the same words see different pages.
