AI SEO Services: Being In The Answer, Not Just The Results

More searches now end with an answer rather than a list. The assistant reads a handful of sources, writes a paragraph, and names a few businesses inside it. If you are not one of the sources it read, you are not in the answer, and the customer may never scroll far enough to find you.

First page rankings on the key phrases you approve, guaranteed.

We watched it happen on a search in this industry

On 18 September 2026 we captured a search for "guaranteed seo las vegas", location set, personalisation off. An AI-written answer appeared above everything else on the page. It explained what SEO guarantees are, noted that Google warns no agency can promise a specific position, and named companies before a single ordinary result appeared.

That is one search, on one day, in one industry. We are not going to inflate it into a trend. What it shows is concrete enough on its own: the surface exists, it sits above everything, and being named in it is a different achievement from ranking below it.

An answer is not a ranking

Ordinary search returns a list and lets the reader choose. An assistant reads a set of sources and writes one response, then cites some of them. That changes what gets rewarded.

Three differences matter in practice.

It quotes rather than links

A page that states something plainly, in a sentence that can be lifted, is easier to quote than a page that takes four paragraphs to get to the point. Clear writing has always been good practice. Here it is the mechanism.

It prefers what it can verify

Figures with a source and a date, rules with a citation, claims that can be checked against something outside your own website. An unsupported assertion is exactly what an assistant has been built to be careful with.

It reads your whole footprint

Not just your site. Your profile, your listings, what other pages say about you, and whether all of it agrees. Contradiction across those sources is a reason to leave you out of an answer.

What we do about it

This is a newer surface. The work is a method of building authority and expertise with helpful content rather than a settled playbook, and what follows is what we do and why, not a promise about what it produces.

Write pages that can be quoted

The answer to the question in the first paragraph, stated plainly, before the explanation. Headings that are questions people actually ask. This is the same discipline that makes a page good to read, which is why it costs nothing to adopt.

Source what can be sourced

Figures with their origin and their year, rules with their citation, and dates on anything that will go stale. On this site we do it for our own pages, which is why the industry pages carry the data year and the statute number.

Keep the footprint consistent

The site, the profile and the listings saying the same thing about what you do, where you are and what you are called. This overlaps with the local work, which is one more reason these are one service.

Watch what the answers actually say

Capturing what assistants return for your phrases, on a date, so there is a record of whether you appear and what is said about you when you do. It is the only measurement available at the moment, and it is worth more than an opinion about the future.

What nobody can tell you yet, including us

How much traffic this will take from ordinary search in two years. Whether the assistants will keep citing sources the way they do now. What share of buyers will act on an answer without ever visiting a website.

Anyone giving you a figure for those is guessing. We would rather say that than sell certainty we do not have.

What we can say is that the work above costs nothing extra here, overlaps almost entirely with work worth doing anyway, and puts you in a surface that already sits above everything else on some searches. That is a reasonable position to take on something still forming.

How the guarantee applies here

The guarantee covers all three kinds of search, this one included.

Said plainly: what counts as achieved is defined for the ordinary rankings, which are a position on a page. An AI answer is not a position, and there is no agreed way to measure placement in one. Ask us during the audit what the guarantee looks like for your market and we will answer it in front of you, rather than write a sentence here that sounds tidier than it is.

What this connects to

Almost all of it is work the other two need anyway. Pages that state things plainly rank better in ordinary search. A consistent footprint wins the map. Sources and dates make a page more useful to a reader as well as to a model. The reason AI search is not a separate invoice here is that separating it would mean charging twice for the same work.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does AI SEO cost?

Nothing on top. Local, organic and AI search are one service here and none of the three is billed separately. What your market takes overall depends on your domain, your existing links and citations, and who is ahead of you now, and you get that number after the audit.

Is this real yet, or is it hype?

Both, depending on who is describing it. The surface is real: we captured an AI answer sitting above every ordinary result on a search in this industry. The hype is in the confident predictions about what share of traffic it will take. We do the work and report what we see.

Can you guarantee I will appear in AI answers?

No. There is no position to hold and no agreed way to measure placement, and anyone promising it is describing something they cannot verify. What we can do is the work that makes a business quotable, and show you what the answers say before and after.

Is this the same as GEO or AEO?

Same subject, different labels. Generative engine optimisation and answer engine optimisation are names for optimising toward AI answers. We use plain words for it because the labels change faster than the practice does.

Will this stop ordinary SEO from mattering?

Not on any evidence available now. Assistants read the web, and what they read is largely what ranks. A business with nothing ranking is not a business an assistant has much to quote.
