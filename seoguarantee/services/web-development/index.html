SEO Web Design and Management: Built To Rank, Kept Current

Nothing pushes a business through the web clutter quite like a unique and impactful website that brings value to customers, and that is something we are experts in. SEO web design is that website, built so a search engine can read it and kept current after it launches.

First page rankings on the key phrases you approve, guaranteed.

A site can be beautiful and still be unrankable

Most of the sites we take over were designed by someone who was never asked about search. They photograph well. They also bury the page title in an image, load the content after the visitor has decided to leave, and repeat the same three paragraphs across forty pages because the template needed filling.

None of that is visible to the owner. All of it is visible to a search engine.

The second problem is slower and more expensive. A site nobody can change stops being updated. Six months later the prices are wrong, the service you stopped offering is still listed, and the only person who can fix it stopped answering emails a year ago.

Website design and development, built for the search as well as the visitor

Built in the same house as the SEO, because a site we cannot change is a guarantee we cannot make. There is no separate build fee and no separate maintenance retainer.

Structure a search engine can read

Headings that describe the page, titles and descriptions written for the phrases in your contract, internal links that lead somewhere, and a URL structure that will still make sense when the site has fifty pages instead of five.

Speed, and the phone first

For most local trades the majority of visits arrive on a phone, often on a bad connection, often while the person is standing in front of the problem they need solved. The site is built for that visit first.

We keep it current, and a change does not come with an invoice

This is the part most agencies leave out of the quote, and it is the reason so many of the sites we take over are two years out of date.

Prices change. Hours change at holidays. You stop offering something, or start offering something new, or hire someone whose name should be on the page. On a site you cannot edit yourself, each of those is an email to whoever built it, a quote, a wait, and eventually a decision that it is not worth the trouble. So the price stays wrong.

We manage the site for as long as you are with us. Content changes, new pages, fixes, the platform kept up to date and backed up. It is in the same contract as everything else, which means nobody has to decide whether a change is worth paying for.

What management covers

Text, prices, hours, staff and services updated on request. New pages built when the business needs one. Broken things fixed. The platform, its dependencies and its backups kept current, which is maintenance nobody notices until it has not been done.

Why it matters to the ranking

A page that has not changed in two years, about a service you no longer sell, is working against the rest of the site. Keeping the site accurate is not housekeeping, it is part of the same job as earning the position in the first place.

What this connects to

The build is where the technical work lands, the search work decides what the pages are for, and the conversion work decides whether the visit turns into a call. All three are in the same contract.

Each kind of search asks something different of the site. Organic rankings need a structure a search engine can read. The map needs location information that agrees with your profile. AI answers need pages that state things plainly enough to be quoted. One build serves all three, which is why it is not a separate project.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does web design and development cost?

There is nothing separate to pay. The website, its development and its ongoing management come with the SEO guarantee, with no build fee and no maintenance retainer. What the whole engagement costs depends on what your market takes, and you get that number after the audit.

Is the website really included?

Yes. Web design and development come with the guarantee, with no separate build fee. You pay for the guarantee.

Do I have to move my site?

That depends on what you have. The audit looks at the site as it stands, including its age, its links and its current authority, because those are part of what decides how long page one takes.

Who owns the site?

You do.

Is ongoing management extra?

No. Updates, new pages and maintenance are in the same contract. There is no change fee and no hourly rate for editing your own business details.

How quickly do changes get made?

Ask us during the audit and we will tell you what to expect. What we will not do is make you raise a ticket to correct your opening hours.
