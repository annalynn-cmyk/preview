SEO Services, With the First Page Guarantee

Expert keyword strategy backed by years of experience, aimed at one outcome: your business on the first page of Google and the other major search engines. This is the part of the Full Suite you are paying for, and the only part that carries the guarantee.

First page rankings on the key phrases you approve, guaranteed.

You are not competing with every business in your trade. You are competing for five slots

Search for a trade and a city and look at what is actually on the screen. Three paid adverts. Then a map with five companies in it. Then Yelp, then Angi. The organic results everybody talks about optimising for begin somewhere below all of that.

We capture this, by hand, for the industries and cities we work in, and the shape changes more than people expect. Search "plumber las vegas" and there are four paid adverts, one of them sitting inside the map itself. Search "water heater repair las vegas" the same afternoon and there are no adverts at all. Same trade, same city, same day.

Which phrase you are found for decides how much of the page you are reaching. That is the work: choosing the phrases where page one is winnable and worth winning, then taking them.

What technical SEO, on-page and local SEO actually involve

No hours logged against a vague retainer. These are the things we do, and they are the same things whether you are in Nevada or Vermont.

Technical SEO

Canonical tags and internal linking structures fixed. Images, code and duplicate content dealt with. Mobile and desktop both treated as the primary device, because for most trades the phone already is. Google Search Console set up properly so the site's own data is available rather than guessed at.

On-page SEO

Meta tags, titles, keywords and page descriptions written for the phrases in your contract, not stuffed with them. Content built around what your customers actually type, which we research and you approve before a word is written.

Local SEO

Google Business Profile setup and management, and the citations and directory work behind it. In most local trades the map sits above every organic result, so a profile that is wrong or unclaimed costs more than a slow website does.

Off-page and authority

Quality high authority backlinks, content marketing and promotion with your site URL, and press releases we write for you. What we do not do is buy links, spin articles or submit your site to a hundred directories nobody reads. That work was sold for years, and it is the reason some sites cannot rank now.

What you get every month

A monthly summary. The list of keywords and phrases being optimised. A page ranking report for Google, Bing, Yahoo and Ask, so the position is a number you can check rather than a claim we make.

The guarantee is measured against the phrases in your contract, within the window your contract sets, so there is never a question of what was promised or when.

How the guarantee applies here

We do not offer it until we have measured your market. The audit looks at who holds page one for your phrases today, how old their domains are, what links and citations they already have, and how strong their authority is. Those four things decide whether page one is a six month job or a twelve month one.

Then we propose the key phrases, you approve them, and the contract covers up to half of them reaching page one within the window. We only make the offer to businesses we can actually get there.

How the guarantee works

What this work depends on

Rankings are not won by the SEO alone. A site a search engine cannot read will not rank whatever we do to the tags, which is why the web design is in the same contract. A page that ranks and loses the visit has cost you money rather than made it, which is why the conversion work is too. And in most local trades the reviews sit directly above the search results, which makes reputation part of the same job.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do SEO services cost?

There is no price list, and an agency with one is quoting before it has looked. What your market takes depends on your domain's age, the links and citations you already have, and who is ahead of you now. The audit gives you the number.

How long does it take?

Six months or twelve, set in the agreement. Which one depends on what the audit finds in your market, not on what you would like to hear.

Do you guarantee the number one position?

No. Nobody controls the exact position of a result, and an agency promising one is either guessing or selling. Page one is something we can work to and measure against.

Do I choose the keywords, or do you?

Both. We research what your customers type and propose the list. You approve every phrase before work starts.
