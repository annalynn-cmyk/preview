How Our SEO Guarantee Works

We measure your market first. Who holds page one for your phrases today, how old their domains are, what links and citations they already have, how strong their authority is. That is what decides whether page one is a short job or a long one, and it is why we do not quote before we look.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can you guarantee first page rankings?

Because we do not offer the guarantee until we have measured what your market takes. The assessment tells us who holds page one, what they have behind them, and what it would cost to get past them. If the answer is that we cannot, we do not make the offer.

Can you guarantee the number one spot?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. Nobody controls the exact position of a result, and a company promising one is either guessing or selling. Page one is a position we can work to and measure against. Number one is a claim.

How much does SEO cost?

There is no price list, and any agency with one is quoting before it has looked. What your market takes depends on your domain's age, the links and citations you already have, and the authority of the companies currently ahead of you. The assessment gives you the number.

Which key phrases does the guarantee cover?

The ones named in your contract. We research what your customers actually type, propose the list, and you approve it before work begins.

What is included at no extra cost?

Branding, web design, graphic design, conversion rate optimization, content development and consulting over the phone. You pay for the guarantee. The rest comes with it.
